Architecture Agent Toolkit

At a glance

Built for evidence, control, and reuse

14portable skills
3coordinated agents

The toolkit separates portable reasoning skills from side-effecting connectors. Agents exchange artifact references through a versioned workspace and explicit handoffs, keeping state inspectable and workflows resumable.

Deterministic validators run inside agent loops and again in CI. Governance verdicts and outbound actions stop at a human approval boundary, with the exact draft shown before anything irreversible occurs.

Files as the handoff bus

Artifacts are inspectable, diffable, resumable, and referenced without copying raw source content between agents.

Validation is reusable

The same deterministic checks run during generation and in CI, reducing drift between workflow and merge-time policy.

People own the boundary

Automation prepares and previews consequential actions; an accountable human approves the final governance decision.

Illustrative workflow traces

From uncertain inputs to reviewable outputs

01

Intake to architecture

Turn a mixed, untrusted architecture corpus into grounded outputs.

  1. Normalise documents and register provenance.
  2. Extract confidence-tagged claims, conflicts, and gaps.
  3. Map evidenced components and relationships.
  4. Produce reviews, diagrams, and measurable fitness functions.
  5. Validate every artifact before handoff.
Done: validated artifact references passed to downstream work.
02

Governance gate

Prepare a governance pack, then stop at the approval boundary.

  1. Read validated architecture handoff references.
  2. Draft decisions and the governance submission pack.
  3. Fail closed when mandatory evidence or a gate is missing.
  4. Prepare a verdict preview without side effects.
  5. Request explicit human approval before finalisation or publication.
Needs approval: a preview is ready; no publication or final verdict occurs.

Evaluation and guardrails

Control is part of the architecture

Evidence-labelled claims

Claims without direct evidence are cited or marked as inferred and awaiting confirmation.

Fail-closed governance

Incomplete governance packs cannot report readiness or pass a decision gate.

Diagram round-trips

Rendered diagrams must preserve the nodes and edges in their source model.

Contained writes

Path validation rejects writes outside the active session workspace.

Human approval

Publication and final verdicts stop for approval without causing a side effect.

Tamper-evident audit

Hash-chain verification exposes changes to later links in the decision log.

Evidence is based on repository code, tests, evaluation fixtures, and architecture records. The results are a point-in-time assessment of the private toolkit; detailed fixtures remain private. No live cloud deployment, external connector, or model-judge run was performed for this extraction.

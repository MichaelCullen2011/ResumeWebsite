About

Edinburgh, UK · michaelcullen2024@gmail.com

Most of my work sits between strategy and implementation: understanding how a complicated system works, deciding what needs to change, and helping people move it in the right direction.

I grew up in London, studied Physics at Loughborough, and moved to Edinburgh in 2021. My MPhys thesis asked whether neutrinos could be used as qubits. The simple answer is not quite, and the exercise taught me to follow an interesting idea far enough to find its limits.

Outside work, I read science fiction, climb, practise taekwondo, play chess, and look for reasons to travel. A trip to Hong Kong in 2013 started the habit; Edinburgh had already won me over by 2015.
